## 01. Professional Direction
My portfolio explores the emerging field of **Neural Interface Design**. As AI moves beyond the screen, I am positioning myself as a product engineer who bridges the gap between Large Language Models (LLMs) and direct human intent. My focus is on creating "Clean Complexity"—interfaces that feel like an extension of the human mind.

## 02. The Design System
The visual identity of this site is built on the **"Museum of Echoes"** framework. 
* **Theme:** Deep Space / Cosmic (The Galaxy background represents the vast, unexplored potential of Neural AI).
* **Animations:** I implemented "Data Signals" (horizontal pulsing lines) to represent the constant flow of neural information between human and machine.
* **Tone:** High-end, professional, and future-facing.

## 03. AI Workflow & Orchestration
This project serves as a case study in **AI-Human Collaboration**. I did not use AI to "do the work," but rather acted as the Creative Director and Lead Architect.

| Phase | AI Tool | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Research** | Gemini 1.5 Pro | Synthesizing job requirements for "Applied AI Engineers" and "Neural Tech" roles to ensure the portfolio's credibility. |
| **Prototyping** | Claude 3.5 Sonnet | Generating high-performance Tailwind CSS components and specialized "Data Signal" animations. |
| **Asset Gen** | Midjourney / DALL-E | Creating the visual assets and conceptual UI mocks for the projects section. |
| **Code Review** | GPT-4o | Troubleshooting the scrollytelling logic and ensuring responsive design for mobile. |

## 04. Aspirational Projects
* **Mnemonic Stream:** A conceptual wearable AI that organizes human memory via sensory cues.
* **Haptic Bridge:** A software interface that translates AI confidence levels into physical touch feedback to solve the "hallucination" problem.

## 05. Research & Planning
Before building, I researched the technical requirements for engineers at companies like **Neuralink**, **Anthropic**, and **OpenAI**. I identified that "Trust" and "Latency" are the biggest hurdles in AI adoption. My portfolio is designed to solve these problems through clear, real-time feedback loops (represented by the animated signals).
